Questions

Do you guarantee a number of leads?

No. We build and run the system and report honestly. Results depend on your market, offer and ad budget, and your team's follow-up speed.

Is ad spend included?

No. Advertising spend (for example Meta ads) is additional and paid by you directly to the platform, so you see exactly where the money goes.

Is there a long contract?

The 90-day program has a fixed scope and price. Monthly plans can be reviewed each month. We will confirm terms in writing before you pay.

Are prices inclusive of GST?

All prices on this page exclude GST.

What is the free AI visibility report?

A report that measures how ready your site is for AI assistants and tests which customer questions your content answers. It does not show what any assistant says today. It is free; we ask for your contact details so we can follow up, only if you tick the consent box.
